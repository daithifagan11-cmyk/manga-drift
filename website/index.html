01 // WHO I AM

About Me

Craft & Vision

I love blending stylized artistic direction with responsive, tactile game feel. Whether tuning suspension and tire slip curves on a mountain hairpin or writing custom GLSL shaders for wet asphalt reflections and manga speed lines, I focus on making every frame feel fast and alive.

What I'm Building

My flagship project, MangaDrift, draws inspiration from classic Japanese touge racing and manga aesthetics—combining inverted-hull ink outlines, dynamic 3D Katakana/Romaji onomatopoeia popups, and combo-based drift scoring right in the browser.

Always Experimenting

From procedural track and environment generation to WebAssembly exports and automated CI/CD deployment pipelines, I enjoy taking projects all the way from initial prototype to playable web release.
